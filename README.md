💡 About ZENEX FinTech

We are Team ZENEX, a team of second-year Electronics & Communication Engineering students from VIT Chennai.

We built ZENEX FinTech, an AI-powered stock analysis application that combines multiple analytical agents to evaluate market conditions and generate actionable BUY, HOLD, or SELL recommendations.

⚙️ How It Works
📈 Market Data
      ↓
🤖 Multi-Agent Analysis
      ↓
Technical • Sentiment • Fundamentals • Risk
      ↓
🧠 AI Signal Synthesis
      ↓
🟢 BUY  |  🔵 HOLD  |  🔴 SELL

The recommendation considers risk profile, investment horizon, confidence, and conflicting signals.
